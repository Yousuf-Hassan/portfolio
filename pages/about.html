About

The person behind the code

A Computer Science student who fell in love with building things for the web — and never stopped learning.

Introduction

I'm Yusuf Xasan, a Computer Science student at the University of Hargeisa — Faculty of Engineering and Computing. I spend most of my time learning how software works — from the first line of HTML to the architecture behind a database — and building projects that turn what I learn into something real and usable.

My journey started with curiosity about how websites are made. That curiosity grew into a passion for full-stack web development: designing clean, accessible interfaces on the frontend and understanding the logic, data, and systems that power them on the backend.

Today I work daily with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, version-control everything with Git and GitHub, and I'm steadily expanding into Node.js, SQL, Java, and Python.

Career Goals

In the short term, I want to deepen my backend skills — building real APIs, working with databases, and shipping full-stack applications end to end. In the longer term, I aim to become a well-rounded software developer who can design a great interface, build a solid system behind it, and contribute meaningfully to teams and open-source projects.

Developer Philosophy

I believe great software is simple, accessible, and honest. Simple code is easier to maintain; accessible interfaces work for everyone; and honest work means shipping what you build, documenting what you learn, and never pretending to know more than you do. I also believe in learning in public — committing early, committing often, and letting the Git history tell the real story of progress.
